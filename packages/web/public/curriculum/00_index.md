## 📖 Book-by-Book Breakdown

### Genki I — 「げんき I」 (Lessons 1–12)

The gold-standard beginner textbook. Covers all fundamental grammar needed for daily conversation.

**Grammar landmarks:**
- L1–2: XはYです, questions, これ/それ/あれ
- L3–4: Verb conjugation (る/う/irregular), ある/いる, past tense
- L5–6: Adjectives (い/な), te-form, requests & permissions
- L7–8: ている form (progressive/resultant), short forms
- L9–10: Past short forms, comparisons, つもり
- L11–12: ～たい, ～たり, ～んです, ～すぎる

### Genki II — 「げんき II」 (Lessons 13–23)

Takes you from "survival Japanese" to expressing opinions, conditions, and social relationships.

**Grammar landmarks:**
- L13–14: Potential verbs, giving/receiving, ～ほしい
- L15–16: Volitional, ～ておく, doing favors (～てあげる/くれる)
- L17–18: ～そうです (seems), conditionals (～と, ～ば, ～なら, ～たら)
- L19–20: Honorific & humble language (intro)
- L21–22: Passive, causative, ～てある
- L23: Causative-passive, ～ことにする/なる, ～まで

### Tobira — 「とびら」 (15 Chapters)

Bridges the gap to authentic Japanese. Each chapter is topic-based (geography, technology, food, history, etc.) with dense grammar notes.

**Grammar categories:**
- Explanatory: ～わけだ, ～わけではない, ～ということだ
- Obligation/necessity: ～べき, ～ざるを得ない, ～ほかない
- Certainty/conjecture: ～に違いない, ～はずだ, ～かもしれない
- Limitation: ～に過ぎない, ～しかない, ～だけ
- Cause/reason: ～せいで, ～おかげで, ～ばかりに
- Time/sequence: ～次第, ～たとたん, ～うちに
- Condition/concession: ～限り, ～としても, ～にしても

## ✅ Quick Self-Check Before Starting

- [ ] Can you read hiragana fluently? → Skip to `02_genki1_grammar.md`
- [ ] Can you read katakana? → Skip to `02_genki1_grammar.md`
- [ ] Can you form ～てください / ～てもいい / ～てはいけません? → You're past Genki I Lesson 6
- [ ] Can you use short forms casually with friends? → You're past Genki I Lesson 8
- [ ] Can you say "if X, then Y" in multiple ways? → You're into Genki II
- [ ] Can you use honorific/humble language? → You're at Genki II Lesson 19+
- [ ] Can you read NHK Easy News without a dictionary? → You're at Tobira level

> **Not sure?** Take the `skill_level.md` placement test. It will pinpoint exactly where to start.
